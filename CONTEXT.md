# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Missed Call Follow-Up | The product: when a call goes unanswered, the caller hears back and the lead lands in a pipeline. TEKGUYZ package A. |
| Sample Business | Keywind Roofing, the made-up roofer in South Florida the demo plays. Not a real company. |
| Caller | The person who called. In the demo, the visitor plays the Caller. |
| Owner | The person at the business who gets the alerts. In the demo, Marisol. |
| Channel | How the Caller hears back: text, email or voice callback. The business picks it. |
| Follow-up | The short exchange after the missed call: three questions (what, where, how soon) and a closing line. |
| Run | One visitor call, from the Call button to the lead (`lib/demo/conversation.ts`). |
| Lead | One Caller's details on the pipeline board. |
| Stage | A column on the board: New, Called back, Quote sent, Won. |
| Alert | A notice to the Owner's phone: one when the call is missed, one when the lead lands. |

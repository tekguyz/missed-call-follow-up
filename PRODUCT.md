# Product

**Who it is for.** Owners of small trade businesses in South Florida: HVAC and AC repair, roofing, boat and marine repair. They are on a job, under a boat or done for the day when the phone rings.

**The problem.** A call that goes unanswered is often a job that goes to the next name on the list. A voicemail nobody turns into a customer does not help.

**The fix (TEKGUYZ package A).** When a call is missed, the caller hears back right away on the channel the business picks: a text, an email or an automated voice callback. It asks what they need, where, and how soon. The lead lands in the business's pipeline or CRM, and the owner gets an alert.

**This repo** is the landing page and the one-click demo that shows that fix end to end. It is not a product a client logs into. A client's real setup is built for their phone system and CRM.

**Voice.** Plain, calm, honest. No prices, no statistics, no setup-time claims, no phone numbers. Never say it is already running for anyone.
